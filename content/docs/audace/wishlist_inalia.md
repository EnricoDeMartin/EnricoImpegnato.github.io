## Spreaders
We would like to measure the amount of vertical bend of the three appendices, to validate our layup as well as the tensions that our rig imparts upon the spreaders.

For reference, the spreaders are rigged with them laying down on the horizontal (X/Y) plane, so the vertical bend of the three appendices is considered to be the one that makes the tip of each appendice go above and below the horizontal plane.

The fixed point is the hole at the center of the three appendices, which bolts to the mast. 
The loads imparted on the spreaders are the tensions of the three wires (shrouds) that go through the tip of each appendice. 

The CAD files are:
- spreaders.3dm
- spreaders.igs
- spreaders.stp

## Wings
We would like to understand the amount of bend of the wings, by measuring the vertical bend along the *wingbars*, that are the thick structural elements at the extreme front and back of the wings, that span all across the Y axis of the wings.

For reference, the wings are rigged with the angled panels on each side of the boat, so the bending that we want to measure is going to be the one that makes the wings *close* on themselves book-style or *open* outward.

The wings are rigged with the short horizontal section of the *wingbars* clamped by screws to the hull.
The loads imparted  on the wings are the upwards pull of the wires (shrouds) that hold our mast, which are tied to two deck eyes bolted approximately at the halfway of the front *wingbars*, as well as the downward push of the weight of our helmsman, who sits on outward most edge of the wing.

The CAD files are:
- wings.3dm
- wings.igs
- wings.stp

## Foils
We would like to measure the vertical bend of the two horizontal foils (main and rudder) that provide the lift necessary for our boat to fly.

For reference, both of the foils are rigged laying down across the horizontal (X/Y) plane, so the vertical bend would be the one that makes the two tips of the wing go above and below the horizontal plane.

The horizontal foils are fixed to the vertical foils in their center, where there's the bulb.
The loads on the foils are the upwards forces applied on the entire horizontal surfaces by the hydrodynamic lift of the profile.

The CAD files are:
- mainfoil.3dm
- mainfoil.igs
- mainfoil.stp
- rudderfoil.3dm
- rudderfoil.igs
- rudderfoil.stp
